# Data Subject Request Runbook

## Purpose

Operate candidate privacy-rights requests without turning the automated workflow into a
legal-decision engine.

## Workflow

1. The authenticated candidate submits an access, portability, rectification, erasure,
   restriction, or objection request.
2. The platform records the request, identity-verification state, submission time and due date.
3. Organization privacy administrators review jurisdiction, exceptions, retention duties,
   legal holds and identity evidence.
4. Access/portability requests can generate an encrypted export from candidate-owned platform
   data after identity verification.
5. Erasure, restriction, objection and rectification requests require administrator review of
   legal/contractual retention duties before completion.
6. Record the resolution rationale without placing unnecessary sensitive data in the notes.
7. Preserve the minimum audit evidence required by applicable law and policy.

The default software due date is 30 days. Legal teams must apply any jurisdiction-specific
calendar rules, extensions, exceptions or statutory requirements.
