# Phase 8 automated evidence

The scheduled evidence collector records hash-addressed snapshots for:

- AI inventory status
- EU AI Act / ISO 42001 implementation-control status
- open/critical AI incident metrics
- existing Phase 7 eval/fairness/review evidence

Evidence automation is **audit preparation**, not a legal opinion, EU conformity assessment,
ISO/IEC 42001 certification, SOC 2 report, or regulator filing.

Recommended production additions:

- export evidence to immutable/WORM storage;
- digitally sign evidence manifests;
- record CI artifact digests and container SBOM/provenance;
- retain management-review and internal-audit approvals;
- connect SIEM case IDs to incident evidence;
- maintain an evidence retention schedule by jurisdiction.
