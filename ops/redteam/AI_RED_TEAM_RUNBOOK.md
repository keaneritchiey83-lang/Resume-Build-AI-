# AI Red-Team Runbook

Phase 9 separates deterministic policy checks from deployment-level red-team exercises.

## Automated cases

- protected-trait ranking must be denied;
- structurally checked credentials must not be promoted to cryptographically verified;
- autonomous job application must be denied.

## Manual/system-execution cases

- prompt injection in retrieved job or career material;
- attempts to exfiltrate provider secrets or another tenant's data;
- cross-tenant authorization attempts;
- unsupported résumé metrics or achievements generated by the model.

Manual cases are recorded as `manual_required`, never automatically passed. Test them in a
controlled environment with representative integrations and realistic identity/tenant
boundaries. Findings should be triaged into human review and the AI incident process when
appropriate.
